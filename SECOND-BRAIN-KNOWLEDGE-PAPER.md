## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. S. Field (1979). Screenplay: The Foundations of Screenwriting. Delta — Canonical three-act structure framework.
2. J. Campbell (1949). The Hero with a Thousand Faces. Pantheon — Monomyth structure underlying narrative analysis.
3. C. Vogler (1992). The Writer's Journey: Mythic Structure for Writers. Michael Wiese Productions — Applied Hero's Journey framework for film analysis.
4. D. Bordwell, K. Thompson (2013). Film Art: An Introduction (10th ed.). McGraw-Hill — Standard mise-en-scène/formal analysis textbook.
5. A. Sarris (1962). Notes on the Auteur Theory in 1962. Film Culture — Foundational auteur theory.
6. R. Stam (2000). Film Theory: An Introduction. Blackwell — Survey of major film-theoretical frameworks.
7. R. Altman (1999). Film/Genre. BFI Publishing — Genre theory for convention-based critique.
8. S. Eisenstein (1949). Film Form: Essays in Film Theory. Harcourt, Brace & World — Montage theory relevant to editing analysis.
9. A. Bazin (1967). What Is Cinema? University of California Press — Realist film-criticism theory.
10. M. McDonald (2004). Film Acting: An Introduction. Routledge — Framework for evaluating screen performance craft.
11. D. Bordwell (1985). Narration in the Fiction Film. University of Wisconsin Press — Formal narrative-structure analysis.
12. K. Thompson (1999). Storytelling in the New Hollywood: Understanding Classical Narrative Technique. Harvard University Press — Structural analysis of contemporary narrative craft.
13. M. Chion (1994). Audio-Vision: Sound on Screen. Columbia University Press — Sound design's role in cinematic meaning.
14. J. Buhler, D. Neumeyer, R. Deemer (2010). Hearing the Movies: Music and Sound in Film History. Oxford University Press — Score/sound evaluation framework.
15. P. Cook (2007). The Cinema Book (3rd ed.). BFI Publishing — Comprehensive film-theory reference.
16. L. Giannetti (2013). Understanding Movies (13th ed.). Pearson — Widely used introductory film-analysis textbook.
17. T. Corrigan (2014). A Short Guide to Writing About Film (9th ed.). Pearson — Practical framework for structured film-review writing.
18. V. F. Perkins (1972). Film as Film: Understanding and Judging Movies. Penguin — Craft-based evaluative film criticism.
19. R. Ebert (2006). Roger Ebert's Movie Yearbook. Andrews McMeel — Widely referenced popular-critical evaluative standards.
20. J. Monaco (2000). How to Read a Film: The World of Movies, Media, and Multimedia. Oxford University Press — Cross-disciplinary film-literacy framework.
